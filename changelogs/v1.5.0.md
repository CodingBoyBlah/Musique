## Apple Music

- Sign in with Apple Music instead of Spotify, with an Apple Music subscription.
- Browse your Apple Music library and playlists, search, open artist pages and start radio.
- Play Apple Music in AAC: High (256 kbps) or Normal (64 kbps), picked in Settings.
- Hear Apple Music tracks at an even volume, levelled by Apple's loudness data.
- Read Apple Music's own synced lyrics, matched to the song from Apple's catalog.
- Edit your own Apple Music playlists, and share songs as Apple Music links.
- Install Google's Widevine module from a dialog the first time it is needed. Apple Music playback depends on it.
- Skip and seek with no wait. The next two tracks load ahead of time.

## Switch between accounts

- Stay signed in to Spotify and Apple Music together, and switch between them from Settings or the title bar.
- Sign out of one service and the other takes over.

## Blends

- Open a Blend and see who is in it, with their faces on the cover and in the header.
- See who each song in a Blend was picked for, shown as a small face on the row.
- Find your Blends on their own shelf in the library, above your playlists.

## Artists and radio

- Start an artist's radio and get Spotify's own radio playlist for them. Open it from the toast, and its page shows Pause while it plays.
- See an artist's latest release beside their popular songs.
- Read monthly listeners and genres on one line under the artist's name.

## Search

- Get quick search results as one list with the best match first, instead of fixed sections by type.
- See placeholder rows while results load and keyboard hints at the bottom of the list.
- Get a better top result on the search page.

## Smoother motion

- Open and close lyrics, the queue and friends without the page stuttering on Home.
- Watch tiles fade and scale in and out when a grid changes, instead of popping.

## macOS

- See a monochrome menu bar icon that matches the other menu bar items.
- Use the system font and a window without a title, like other Mac apps.
- Open Settings with Cmd+,.
