## Lyrics

- Prefer Apple Music lyrics and check their timing against Spotify's synced lyrics.
- Preserve Apple Music's lyric text while borrowing matching line and word timings from other providers.
- Support untimed TTML lyrics and overlapping line endings.
- Recheck cached Apple Music results and prevent lyric source switches from replacing the preferred text.
- Improved lyric row timing in the lyrics panel and immersive mode.

## Spotify compatibility

- Retry requests with smaller result limits when Spotify rejects the requested endpoint limit.
- Read playlists using Spotify's newer API fields, with a fallback to internal playlist data.
- Recommend custom Spotify credentials in Settings and prompt users on shared access to configure their own.
